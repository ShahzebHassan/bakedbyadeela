# Direction contract — client-led floral portfolio redesign

The user explicitly selected the Olive & Mac reference and supplied a screenshot. Preserve Baked By Adeela's pastel palette and use original photography. This is a code-led implementation of that pinned direction, not an open concept exercise.

## First viewport
One full-width blush header, approximately 126px on desktop / 100px on mobile. Left-aligned typographic BAKED by / Adeela wordmark. Serif navigation and restrained enquiry control. No announcement strip or circular logo. Below it, a full-width three-photo portfolio across desktop, two readable photographs on mobile; wedding collection remains prominent in the page content and mobile navigation. Small ivory caption links sit on the imagery. Centre-aligned editorial introduction follows, with Birmingham/Midlands and hand-piped buttercream flowers in readable prose.

## Visitor path and interaction
Browse collections and individual designs; select a product to open the one enquiry page with category and design prefilled. Netlify submits the enquiry. No form is shown on home, collection or contact pages. Every page can reach the form through the shared header/footer. Original images lead sections; a portrait introduces the maker. An Instagram portfolio section upgrades to live media only when an authorised backend responds.

## Craft
Cormorant Garamond / DM Sans served locally. Warm ink on pastel fields, square photo crops, 1px rules, no app-style cards. Photo hover is a subtle 650ms ease-out scale; reduced-motion removes it. Menus have keyboard/Escape support, native form labels/constraints, visible focus, truthful loading/error states. Desktop/mobile screenshot evidence required.
